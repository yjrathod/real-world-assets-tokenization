## 💡 What are Real-World Assets (RWAs) in Web3?

**Real-World Assets (RWAs)** refer to physical, tangible, or traditional financial assets that are brought on-chain via smart contracts and digital tokens. Instead of relying entirely on native crypto assets, RWA protocols create a digital twin or fractional ownership model of real-world value.

---

### 🏢 Common Types of Tokenized RWAs

* **Real Estate:** Commercial properties, residential housing, or land tokenized into fractional shares to allow retail participation and instant secondary trading.
* **Fixed Income & Debt:** Treasury bills (T-Bills), corporate bonds, private credit, and invoice factoring.
* **Commodities:** Precious metals (gold, silver), agricultural produce, oil, and raw materials backed by audited warehouse receipts.
* **Alternative Assets:** Fine art, luxury watches, revenue-sharing agreements, and verified carbon credit offsets.

---

### ⚙️ How the RWA Tokenization Lifecycle Works

1. **Asset Identification & Legal Structuring:** An off-chain asset is placed under a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) or legal trust to ensure that the token legally represents real ownership or revenue rights.
2. **Document & Reserve Verification:** Property deeds, audit reports, and appraisals are uploaded to decentralized storage (IPFS) and connected via Chainlink Proof-of-Reserve (PoR).
3. **On-Chain Tokenization:** Smart contracts mint compliant fractional tokens (ERC-3643 or permissioned ERC-20) representing ownership shares.
4. **Compliance & Identity (KYC/AML):** Only verified, allowlisted wallets are permitted to hold or trade the tokens to comply with regulatory standards.
5. **Yield & Revenue Distribution:** Off-chain cash flows (e.g., tenant rent, interest payments) are deposited into an automated dividend contract and distributed to token holders in stablecoins.

---

### 🚀 Why Tokenize RWAs?

* **Fractionalization & Liquidity:** Transforms high-value, illiquid assets into micro-shares accessible to everyday investors.
* **24/7 Instant Settlement:** Eliminates intermediary paperwork, broker delays, and cross-border settlement friction.
* **Programmable Cash Flows:** Automates dividend distributions, voting rights, and legal compliance directly within code.
* **Immutable Transparency:** Verifiable ownership records, real-time audit trails, and transparent reserve validation on the blockchain.
